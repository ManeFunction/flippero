0.9:
Beta release, the first public version.
- Text, QR code, QR + text and icon + text labels with a framed preview of what will print.
- Connects to the printer through an ESP32 bridge over the GPIO header.
- On-screen keyboard with punctuation (URLs, e-mail addresses).
- Printer info: model, battery and status.
- App info screen with a QR code to the project page.
